# Controls

"Immersive motion controls" is the default control scheme. You can spray graffiti by grabbing the spray can at your hip, pointing, and pulling the trigger. Espresso is activated by taking the coffee at your hip and bringing it to your mouth. The parachute is activated by reaching over your shoulder, grabbing, and pulling (the exact location for the parachute can be set in the VR settings menu).

If your controller reports finger tracking support to SteamVR (like the Steam Frame or Valve Index controllers) the flip it glove is activated by making a thumbs up or middle finger gesture. If not, use the thumbs up and flip off buttons in the table below.

To enable button shortcuts instead of gestures, choose "Motion controls w/ button shortcuts" under VR Settings > Input method. To use a traditional controller, choose "Gamepad".

Button shortcuts:

| Action | Steam Frame | Valve Index | Vive | Oculus | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Left stick | Left stick | Left trackpad | Left stick | Left stick |
| Turn | Right stick | Right stick | Right trackpad | Right stick | Move your head |
| Jump | A | Right A | Right trackpad center click | A | A |
| Pause | Right menu | Hold right A | Right menu | Hold A | Start |
| Recenter | Right stick click | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |
| Thumbs up | D-pad up | Left A | Left trackpad up | X | Left trigger |
| Flip off | D-pad down | Left B | Left trackpad down | Y | Right trigger |
| Spray graffiti | B | Right B | Right trackpad up | B | Y |
| Deploy parachute | X | Left stick click | Left trackpad left | Left stick click | B |
| Use espresso | Y | Right stick click | Left trackpad right | Right stick click | X |
